Why StoryCast exists

Helping young people see the opportunity in agriculture.

StoryCast uses storytelling to explore how agricultural skills, technology, and entrepreneurship could help young people in Rwanda pursue new livelihood opportunities.

The challenge we want to address

Youth unemployment is a complex challenge that cannot be solved by one sector or intervention alone. Agriculture offers several possible pathways, including farming, agribusiness, processing, distribution, marketing, and technology-enabled services.

Yet some young people may see agriculture as traditional work with limited opportunities. StoryCast aims to broaden that conversation by showing the range of skills and business activities connected to the sector.

These opportunities depend on practical conditions, including training, resources, access to finance, infrastructure, and markets. Stories should encourage exploration while recognizing these challenges.

Our mission

Connect young people with agricultural opportunity.

The broader mission behind StoryCast is to help reduce youth unemployment in Rwanda by encouraging young people to explore agriculture and technology, develop practical skills, and find pathways into agricultural employment or entrepreneurship.

What this mission could involve

  • Agricultural training that builds practical, relevant skills.
  • Introducing young people to digital tools and technology that address agricultural needs.
  • Mentorship and guidance for people exploring agricultural businesses.
  • Connections to employers, farmers, agricultural organizations, and potential markets.
  • Stories that help young people learn about different career and entrepreneurship paths.

Inclusive by design

Accessibility matters

StoryCast should be usable by people with different abilities, devices, and content preferences. Accessibility is part of the design and development process, not an afterthought.

Captions

Videos should provide synchronized captions for dialogue and meaningful audio.

Transcripts

Written transcripts provide an alternative way to access the spoken story.

Keyboard access

Navigation and interactive controls should work without requiring a mouse.

Readable design

Clear headings, sufficient contrast, and responsive layouts help visitors navigate the content.
